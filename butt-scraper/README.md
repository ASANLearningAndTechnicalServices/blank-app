# Butt Scraper

Gary's last piece is stuck. Seen from behind, with a giant cartoon butt filling the top of the screen, you pick from a tray of increasingly ridiculous tools to get it out, without hurting Gary too much.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone). No build step and no install; everything, including the sound, is generated in the page.

## How it plays

- **30 stages**, each with its own poop type (frozen, slimy, iron, crystal, haunted...) that is weak to some tools and resists others. The stage card and the top bar show what works.
- **31 tools**, from a feather (tickle him until it giggles loose) and toilet paper up to a hair dryer, plunger, screwdriver, nail file, ghost pepper, vacuum, chef's knife, bow and arrow, rubber duck launcher, woodpecker, beaver, magnet, angle grinder, sledgehammer, sonic blaster, flamethrower, chainsaw, leaf blower, dynamite, excavator, laser, exorcism bell and a black hole gun.
- **Two meters**: *Stuck* (how firmly the poop is lodged) and *Ouch* (Gary's pain). Hit the skin with sharp or powerful tools and he yells; at 100% he faints and you retry.
- Later stages add swinging, hiding, clenching, oozing back, dodging and a three-form final boss.
- Up to 3 stars per stage, based on time left and Gary's pain. Progress is saved in the browser.

## Controls

- Mouse or touch. Drag, hold or tap depending on the tool; the hint above the tool tray says which.
- Keys `1`-`5` switch tools, `Esc` or `P` pauses.
- Sound effects, music and Gary's spoken voice can each be switched off on the title or pause screen.
