# Butt Scraper

Gary's last piece is stuck. Seen from behind, with a giant cartoon butt filling the top of the screen, you pick from a tray of increasingly ridiculous tools to get it out, without hurting Gary too much.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone). No build step and no install: graphics, music and sound are all inside that one file.

## How it plays

- **Tools physically change the poop.** It is made of material that each tool removes in its own way:
  - arrows punch holes, and a row of holes that joins up snaps it
  - the screwdriver drills big round holes
  - the knife and laser slice it
  - the toothbrush scratches grooves and the nail file grinds it down
  - the pig nibbles it and the beaver chews it into an hourglass
  - termites tunnel through it
  - the hammer and rubber ducks crack it
  - the hair dryer and flamethrower melt it
  - water and soda wash and fizz it away
  - the grinder and chainsaw gouge through it
  - dynamite blasts craters in it
- **Anything you cut free falls off** into the toilet. Cut it free near the top (above the dotted guide) and the whole thing drops.
- **Loosening tools** (feather, plunger, fishing rod, magnet, excavator, air horn, baked beans, ghost pepper, exorcism bell) shake, pull or push it until it slides out. Pulling hard on a poop full of holes rips it apart at the weakest point.
- **Every poop is a puzzle.** Weaknesses start hidden as `?`. Gary drops a hint at the start, and trying tools reveals what works ("WEAKNESS FOUND: HEAT!"). What you learn is remembered.
- **Pick your kit.** Before each stage you choose up to 2 to 5 tools from everything unlocked so far, and each has limited uses (6 arrows, a roll of toilet paper that runs out, a hair dryer that overheats).
- **Crusted poops** (6 stages) have a shell of a different material. Damage it with the right tool until it cracks off, then switch to a tool that suits the core.
- **Gary fights back.** Pain makes him squirm and swing the poop. Scary tools make him flinch and clench. Big ouches cost you time, and every chunk you drop buys some back.
- **The poop adapts** from stage 14: keep using one tool and it gets less effective, so switch now and then.
- **Two poops** in stages 16, 21 and 25: a second one slides out after the first.
- **Bathroom chaos** from stage 8: someone bangs on the door, the lights go out (you get a flashlight), the toilet overflows, or Gary's phone rings.
- **Star goals.** One star for clearing, plus two for the stage's goals, such as "Keep Gary's pain under 30%", "Use 2 tools or fewer" or "Drop it in one piece".
- **30 stages and 35 tools.** Later stages add swinging, hiding, clenching, healing, dodging, and a three-form final boss.
- **Two meters:** *Stuck* and *Ouch*. Hit the skin with sharp or powerful tools and Gary yells. At 100% Ouch he faints and you retry.
- Progress, stars and discovered weaknesses are saved in the browser.
- To try any stage without playing through, open **Stages** and tap **Unlock all stages**. **Reset progress** (tap twice) locks them again and clears stars.

## Controls

- Mouse or touch. Drag, hold, tap or aim, depending on the tool. The hint above the tool tray says which.
- Keys `1`-`5` switch tools. `Esc` or `P` pauses.
- Sound effects, music and Gary's voice can each be switched off on the title or pause screen.
