How to play
  • 3D prototype. The camera follows behind you: push up on the stick to run the way you're facing, and the camera swings round as you turn. The map in the corner fills in as you explore. Characters are placeholder models for now.
  • Share one phone. The screen splits in two: your half follows your character and is also your joystick, so drag anywhere on it to steer. With the phone upright and flat between you, Player 2's half is turned to face them. On a keyboard, Player 1 uses WASD and Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
  • You start at opposite ends of a big building. Three toilets stand along the middle, but only one works. The others are out of order. Find the real one first. The loser poops their pants on the spot.
  • Lost? After a while an arrow on the edge of your view points to the working toilet.
  • Every toilet has a guardian. In the office a boss grabs you with "urgent" work, and in the mall a lost tourist asks for directions. Wiggle the stick (or mash keys) to break free faster. At the festival a guard dog barks you away, so wait for it to nap.
  • Grab a rare golden toilet roll to refill both meters and slow your urgency for 10 seconds.
  • Each player's restroom key hangs on a giant keychain somewhere in their half of the building. The working toilet stays locked until you bring yours.
  • No second player? Pick Me vs the computer. The computer has to search for its key and the right toilet too.
  • Career: beat the computer on 9 levels that get bigger and harder. Earn up to 3 stars per level by finishing with calm meters. Stars unlock new characters.
  • Watch the meters on your side: bladder and bowels. If either one empties, you have an accident and you're out.
  • Puddles are slippery and bananas send you spinning. Janitors, roombas, goats and mall cops knock you back. Bump your rival out of the way.
  • Pickups: coffee and chili give a burst of speed but drain your bladder or bowels. A cork calms both meters.
